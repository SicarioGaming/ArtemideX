**Fixes the crash when opening a PC's games.** In 0.1.0, Starwire closed a moment after the list of games appeared, right as the first cover art arrived. It happened on every device and host. Sorry to everyone who hit it on day one, and thank you for the quick reports!

### What changed
- Cover art now loads without crashing. The screen picks up each cover's colour as intended.
- A new test opens the real games screen and feeds it cover art the way a host does, so this can't slip through again.

### Install
Download **Starwire-0.1.2.apk** below and open it. It installs over 0.1.0 and keeps your pairing and settings.

### Check your download
- APK SHA-256: `APK_SHA256`
- Signing certificate SHA-256: `CERT_SHA256` (the same certificate as every Starwire release)
